About TechFest

GITAM TechFest is one of the largest annual college festivals bringing together technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and culture under one roof. Students from various universities compete, collaborate, and showcase their skills.

Coding competitions, AI challenges, Hackathons, Robotics, Web Development, Cyber Security and much more.

No. Registration is completely free for all participants.

Yes. All participants will receive certificates, and winners will receive trophies and prizes.
